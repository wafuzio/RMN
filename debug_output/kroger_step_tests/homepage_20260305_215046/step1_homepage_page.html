Overview

Welcome!

Welcome to our Privacy Request Center!

We offer consumers the right to:

  • Opt-out of the use of their personal data for targeted advertising
  • Opt-out of the sale of their personal data
  • Withdraw consent to use sensitive data

You can learn about your rights to opt out and how to exercise your rights here in our Privacy Request Center by clicking on the applicable tabs.

Sales/targeted advertising

NO

Opt-out of sales/targeted advertising

We use cookies and other tracking technologies ("Tracking Technologies") on our websites that may make your personal data available to third parties in a manner that is considered "targeted advertising" or a "sale" under applicable consumer privacy laws.

To opt-out of sales and targeted advertising using Tracking Technologies on this website, please slide the toggle above to the left, and it will display YES to indicate that you have opted out. If you later change your mind, you can opt back in by sliding the toggle to the right so that it says NO. If an opt-out preference signal has been detected, we have automatically opted you out.

Please Note:

  • This opt-out only applies to the website that you have accessed this opt-out from.
  • Because of the way our opt-out technology currently works, you will also need to opt-out if you access this website from other browsers or devices.
  • Cookie-enabled opt-out signals may no longer be effective if you block or clear cookies.
  • Opting-out will not affect our ability to personalize your experience on our website. You may still receive advertisements that do not rely on targeted advertising or sales.


Opt out of other sales/targeted advertising

As a consumer you may have engaged with us in other ways than just visiting our websites or using our mobile apps. For example, we may collect data about you when you participate in one of our loyalty programs.

In some cases, we may disclose data that we collect about you by means other than Tracking Technologies in a way that may be considered:

  • A "sale" under applicable consumer privacy laws; or
  • Targeted advertising under applicable consumer privacy laws.

If you would like us to update our internal records so that we do not disclose such personal data collected in contexts outside of Tracking Technologies for these purposes, please submit your request(s) using the following links:

  • Sales opt-out click here
  • Targeted advertising opt-out click here

You may also submit your request(s) by calling us at: 1-800-KROGERS (1-800-576-4377).
